K7 FITNESS STUDIO & GYM: WEBSITE IMAGE PROMPTS (MANDATORY IMAGES ONLY)
=====================================================================

HOW TO USE (read once)
----------------------
1. Start a NEW ChatGPT chat. Paste the "MASTER STYLE GUIDE" block first and wait for "ready".
2. Then paste ONE image prompt at a time (1, 2, 3, 4a…). Each prompt is fully self-contained and
   repeats the colour theme, so results stay consistent even in a long chat.
3. If a result is close but not right, use the "FIX-IT PHRASES" at the bottom instead of starting over.
4. Download each image as PNG/JPG and upload it in the Admin panel where it says "Upload to".
   The admin automatically compresses and resizes images, so no editing is needed.
5. Generate 2–3 versions of the HERO image and pick the strongest. It's the most important image.

Deliberately NOT included: Trainer, Gallery and Testimonial photos must be REAL photos of your
coaches, members and gym. AI-generated people there would mislead visitors.

You are my professional fitness photographer and art director. I'm creating the images for the
website of "K7 FITNESS STUDIO & GYM", a premium, modern gym in Tamil Nadu, India. Its brand identity
is POWERFUL, PREMIUM, DARK, ATHLETIC, MODERN: aggressive but clean.

Apply ALL of these rules to EVERY image I request in this chat:

PHOTOGRAPHIC STYLE
- Ultra-photorealistic, editorial campaign quality (the look of a Nike Training or Gymshark
  campaign shot on a full-frame camera such as a Sony A7R V / Canon R5).
- Cinematic, low-key lighting: mostly deep shadow with controlled, sculpted highlights.
- Sharp focus on the subject, natural shallow depth of field, very subtle film grain.
- Realistic skin texture, real sweat, real chalk; no plastic, airbrushed or CGI look.

COLOUR THEME (strict)
- Dominant: deep black #050505 and charcoal #0D0D0F–#151518 (about 60–70% of the frame).
- Single accent colour: crimson red #A10825 (dark crimson #790F1F in haze/shadows, bright crimson
  #D20A35 only on the brightest rim highlights), about 10–15% of the frame.
- Metal and highlights in neutral silver/grey and white.
- NO blue, cyan, purple, teal, green, orange or yellow light anywhere. No rainbow, no neon signs.
- Colour grade: cool-neutral blacks, rich crimson, natural warm skin tones.

LOCATION
- The same premium gym every time: black squat racks and benches, black rubber-hex dumbbells,
  dark grey rubber flooring, concrete or matte black walls, thin crimson LED accent strips along
  walls/ceiling edges, moody overhead spotlights. Clean, uncluttered, expensive-looking.

PEOPLE
- Indian / South Indian adults aged roughly 22–40, athletic and realistic (lean and strong,
  NOT extreme bodybuilders), focused and confident, correct exercise form.
- Clothing: plain black or charcoal athletic wear. No visible brands, logos or slogans.
- Anatomically correct: natural hands with five fingers, realistic faces, correct grip on equipment.

ABSOLUTELY FORBIDDEN IN EVERY IMAGE
- Any text, letters, numbers, words, logos, brand marks, watermarks, signage, posters or screens
  with writing. Weight plates must be blank (no numbers).
- Mirror reflections showing extra people, distorted equipment, bent barbells, floating objects,
  extra limbs or fingers, duplicated people, cartoon/illustration style, over-saturated colours.

When you understand, reply only "ready" and wait for my first image request.

FIX-IT PHRASES  (reply with these when a result needs adjusting)

  "Regenerate exactly the same scene but with absolutely no text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere,
  including on weight plates and walls."

  "Regenerate with ONLY deep black, charcoal and crimson red #A10825 lighting. Remove all blue,
  purple, green and orange light."
- Too dark:
  "Keep the same moody style, but lift the shadows slightly so the people and equipment are clearly
  visible."
- Too bright / not premium:
  "Make it darker and more cinematic: low-key lighting, deeper blacks, stronger crimson rim light."
- Subject in the wrong place (hero / share image):
  "Move the athlete to the right third and keep the left half empty and dark for text."
- Hands, face or equipment look wrong:
  "Regenerate with anatomically correct hands (five fingers, natural grip) and a straight, realistic
  barbell."
- Looks fake / CGI:
  "Make it more photorealistic: natural skin texture, real sweat, subtle film grain, like a real
  camera photo, not a 3D render."
- Inconsistent set:
  "Match the gym, lighting and colour grade of the previous image exactly."
